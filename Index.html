Research Focus

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at Baylor College of Medicine (BCM) specializing in robust bioinformatics solutions for high-throughput sequencing data. My work bridges the gap between complex transcriptomic datasets and biological discovery through scalable pipeline development.

  • Regulatory Networks: Advanced implementation of SCENIC for gene regulatory network inference and regulon analysis.
  • Temporal Dynamics: Utilizing Mfuzz soft clustering to identify patterns in longitudinal expression profiling.
  • Alternative Splicing: Large-scale quantification and visualization of isoform variation using rMATS.
  • Transcriptomics: Comprehensive analysis of RNA-seq and PolyA-seq data to uncover differential expression patterns.
