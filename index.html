Research Focus

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at Baylor College of Medicine (BCM) specializing in high-throughput single-cell and spatial omics, computational biology, machine learning, and scalable bioinformatics pipeline architecture. My research focuses on dissecting cellular heterogeneity, gene regulatory mechanisms, alternative splicing, and post-transcriptional dynamics across high-dimensional genomic datasets.

  • Single-Cell & Spatial Omics: End-to-end sc/snRNA-seq processing, batch correction with Harmony, metacell modeling via SEACells, and automated cell-type annotation with CellTypist.
  • Gene Regulatory Networks & Trajectories: Inferring regulon dynamics using SCENIC / pySCENIC, pseudobulk differential analysis via DESeq2, and lineage trajectory tracing.
  • Transcriptomic Profiling & APA: Comprehensive quantification of RNA-seq, targeted PolyA-seq, and Alternative Polyadenylation (APA) patterns to characterize post-transcriptional regulation.
  • Alternative Splicing: Large-scale isoform variation discovery and differential splicing quantification using rMATS-turbo.
  • Temporal Dynamics & Machine Learning: Pattern extraction and soft clustering across longitudinal time-series data using Mfuzz alongside predictive modeling with Scikit-learn.
  • Epigenomics & Functional Genomics: Profiling chromatin accessibility (ATAC-seq), CUT&RUN, genomic track computation with deepTools, and motif enrichment using HOMER.
