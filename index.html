Research Focus

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at Baylor College of Medicine (BCM) specializing in high-throughput single-cell and spatial omics, computational biology, machine learning, and scalable bioinformatics pipeline architecture. My research bridges high-dimensional multi-omics integration (transcriptomics, epigenomics, and genomics) with translational biomarker discovery, cellular heterogeneity mapping, and post-transcriptional RNA regulation.

  • Single-Cell & Spatial Omics: End-to-end sc/snRNA-seq processing, batch correction with Harmony, metacell aggregation via SEACells, automated annotation using CellTypist, and spatial microenvironment deconvolution.
  • Gene Regulatory Networks & Trajectories: Inferring transcription factor regulon dynamics with SCENIC / pySCENIC, pseudobulk differential analysis via DESeq2, and lineage trajectory tracing.
  • Integrative Multi-Omics: Multi-modal cross-talk resolving bulk RNA-seq, Whole Exome/Genome Sequencing (WES/WGS), ATAC-seq chromatin accessibility, and PolyA-seq for Alternative Polyadenylation (APA).
  • Post-Transcriptional Splicing: Large-scale isoform variation discovery and differential splicing quantification using rMATS-turbo.
  • AI/ML & Temporal Dynamics: Supervised/unsupervised machine learning (random forests, regularized regression, PCA/UMAP) for risk stratification alongside soft time-series clustering with Mfuzz.
  • Epigenomics & Functional Genomics: CUT&RUN profiling, genomic track computation with deepTools, and motif enrichment using HOMER.
