Research Focus

Decoding gene regulatory mechanisms & tumor-immune heterogeneity

I am a computational biologist developing scalable bioinformatics workflows, statistical models, and artificial intelligence frameworks to interrogate complex disease genomics. My research bridges high-dimensional multi-omics integration (transcriptomics, epigenomics, and genomics) with translational biomarker discovery, cellular heterogeneity mapping, and post-transcriptional RNA regulation.

Single-Cell & Spatial Omics:

End-to-end sc/snRNA-seq processing, batch correction with Harmony, metacell aggregation via SEACells, and automated cell-type annotation with CellTypist.

Regulatory Networks & Trajectories:

Reconstructing transcription factor regulons using pySCENIC, pseudobulk differential analysis via DESeq2, and pseudotime lineage tracing.

Integrative Multi-Omics:

Harmonizing bulk RNA-seq, Whole Exome/Genome Sequencing (WES/WGS), ATAC-seq chromatin dynamics, and PolyA-seq for Alternative Polyadenylation (APA).

Alternative Splicing:

Isoform variation discovery and alternative splicing event quantification across complex clinical cohorts using rMATS-turbo.
