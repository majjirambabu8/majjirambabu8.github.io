Research Focus & Overview

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at Baylor College of Medicine (BCM) specializing in high-throughput single-cell and spatial omics, computational biology, machine learning, and scalable bioinformatics pipeline engineering. My research bridges high-dimensional multi-omics integration (transcriptomics, epigenomics, and genomics) with translational biomarker discovery, cellular heterogeneity mapping, and post-transcriptional RNA regulation.

Single-Cell & Spatial Omics End-to-end sc/snRNA-seq processing, batch correction with Harmony, metacell aggregation via SEACells, and automated cell-type annotation with CellTypist.
Regulatory Networks & Trajectories Inferring transcription factor regulon dynamics with pySCENIC, pseudobulk differential analysis via DESeq2, and lineage trajectory tracing.
Integrative Multi-Omics & Variants Harmonizing bulk RNA-seq, Whole Exome/Genome Sequencing (WES/WGS), ATAC-seq chromatin accessibility, and PolyA-seq for Alternative Polyadenylation (APA).
Post-Transcriptional Splicing Isoform variation discovery and alternative splicing event quantification across complex clinical cohorts using rMATS-turbo.
