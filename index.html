Research Focus

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at Baylor College of Medicine specializing in high-throughput single-cell and spatial omics, computational biology, and scalable bioinformatics pipeline engineering. My research focuses on integrating multi-omics data—spanning transcriptomics, epigenomics, and genomics—to map cellular heterogeneity, infer gene regulatory networks, and discover translational biomarkers.

  • Single-Cell & Spatial Omics: Processing sc/snRNA-seq datasets from raw reads to downstream states, batch integration with Harmony, metacell modeling via SEACells, and automated cell-type annotation with CellTypist.
  • Gene Regulatory Networks: Reconstructing transcription factor regulons using pySCENIC, lineage trajectory analysis, and pseudobulk differential expression using DESeq2.
  • Multi-Omics Integration: Harmonizing bulk RNA-seq, Whole Exome/Genome Sequencing (WES/WGS), ATAC-seq, and targeted 3′ PolyA-seq for alternative polyadenylation (APA).
  • Alternative Splicing: Quantifying isoform switches and differential splicing events using rMATS-turbo.
  • Machine Learning & Statistics: Supervised and unsupervised learning (Scikit-learn) for patient stratification, biomarker feature selection, and soft time-course clustering via Mfuzz.
  • Epigenomics & Functional Genomics: Profiling chromatin accessibility (ATAC-seq), CUT&RUN, genomic coverage calculations via deepTools, and transcription factor motif discovery via HOMER.

Computational Workflows & Pipelines

Single-Cell & Spatial Transcriptomics Pipeline

Cell Ranger · Seurat · Scanpy · Harmony · SEACells · CellTypist · pySCENIC

End-to-end framework processing raw FASTQ data to count matrices, resolving batch variance with Harmony, aggregating sparse profiles into metacells via SEACells, running automated annotation with CellTypist, and inferring transcription factor regulon activity.

Differential Expression & Omics Harmonization Suite

DESeq2 · limma · PyBEDTools · pheatmap · ggplot2

Automated pipelines for standard and pseudobulk differential gene expression, multi-dataset identifier harmonization, genomic coordinate intersections, and publication-ready statistical graphics.
