Research Focus

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at Baylor College of Medicine (BCM) specializing in high-throughput transcriptomics, computational biology, and custom bioinformatics pipeline architecture. My research focuses on dissecting gene regulatory mechanisms, alternative splicing, and alternative polyadenylation across large-scale genomic datasets.

  • Gene Regulatory Networks: Inferring regulon dynamics and single-cell network architectures using SCENIC / pySCENIC.
  • Transcriptomic Profiling & APA: Comprehensive quantification of RNA-seq, PolyA-seq, and Alternative Polyadenylation (APA) patterns to characterize post-transcriptional dynamics.
  • Alternative Splicing: Large-scale isoform variation discovery and differential splicing quantification using rMATS-turbo.
  • Temporal Dynamics & Machine Learning: Pattern extraction and soft clustering across longitudinal time-series data using Mfuzz and machine learning approaches.
  • Epigenomics & Motif Analysis: CUT&RUN data integration, genomic track computation with deepTools, and transcription factor motif enrichment using HOMER.
